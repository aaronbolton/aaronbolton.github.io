Aaron Bolton

Infrastructure Technical Architect · Sheffield, UK

I'm an enthusiastic advocate for open source software and self-hosted solutions — I'd rather run something myself and understand how it works than rely on a black box. Most of my free time goes into home lab experiments: multiple Linux distros on the go at once, Docker and virtualisation setups, self-hosted services replacing the cloud ones I used to depend on, and generally pulling things apart to see how they tick.

I'm also into electronics design and building software and hardware solutions end to end — there's a particular satisfaction in taking an idea from a rough sketch to something physical or working on my desk. Alongside that, I've become a keen AI advocate, exploring practical, real-world ways AI tools can actually be useful rather than just hype.

Professionally, I work as an IT Infrastructure Technical Architect, with experience spanning cloud infrastructure, identity and access management, infrastructure support, service resilience, and technical governance. That day job — designing, securing, and improving the infrastructure services everything else depends on — feeds directly into the personal projects, and vice versa. The line between "work skills" and "hobby" has blurred a long time ago.

I like understanding systems properly — how they're built, how they fail, and how to make them better — whether that's a production cloud environment or a Raspberry Pi on my desk.
